Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about licensing and usage.

Is this really a one-time purchase? ▾

Yes. There are zero subscriptions, zero recurring monthly fees, and zero hidden costs. You own your license key permanently for the life of the version.

Can I transfer my license to a new PC? ▾

Yes. Simply click "[ DEACTIVATE KEY ]" inside RetroFlux before switching computers. This releases your hardware seat so you can activate on your new device immediately.

Does it require an internet connection to run? ▾

No. RetroFlux operates 100% offline. After initial activation, your license is verified locally using a machine-bound cryptographic token. It never uploads data or phones home.

Do I need to install FFmpeg or Python separately? ▾

No. The Desktop Edition is a single, self-contained standalone executable (RetroFlux-v1.3.0.exe) with the complete Python runtime and embedded FFmpeg binary bundled inside.
