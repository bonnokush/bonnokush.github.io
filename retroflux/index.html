Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about keys, activation, and usage.

How do I receive my unique license key? ▾

Immediately after checkout, our automated system generates your unique cryptographic license key and emails it directly to your purchase inbox. You can also view it on your post-purchase receipt page.

Does RetroFlux require an internet connection? ▾

No. RetroFlux operates completely air-gapped. Once you enter your key, it verifies locally using an embedded cryptographic checksum. It never phones home or uploads data to any server.

Do I need to install Python or FFmpeg on Windows? ▾

No. The Desktop Edition is distributed as a single portable Windows executable (`RetroFlux.exe`) with the complete runtime and FFmpeg binaries bundled inside. Double-click to launch anywhere.

What is the difference between Desktop and All-Access? ▾

The Desktop Edition provides a Windows PC license for unlimited conversions on desktop. The All-Access Master Bundle delivers both the Windows `.exe` and Android `.apk`, along with full multi-platform unlock across all your devices.
